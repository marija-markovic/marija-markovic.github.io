About

I am a Senior Research Engineer at the Electric Power Research Institute (EPRI), where I work at the intersection of power systems, data analytics, and AI/ML. I currently co-lead DCFlex load forecasting research focused on data center electricity demand growth and its implications for utility planning. Previously, I completed my Ph.D. in Electrical, Computer, and Energy Engineering at the University of Colorado Boulder.
